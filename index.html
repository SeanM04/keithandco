About Us

Experts in event infrastructure & project management

With more than 10 years of experience, Keith & Co Consulting (Pty) Ltd has been delivering some of the best project management in the event industry across South Africa. We conceptualise, design and develop our products and services to provide quality results that meet global standards.

Events are complex, dynamic and ever-changing, and pressure rises as the date approaches. Our job is to take that pressure off you by organising and managing the structure and dynamics of your event on your behalf.
