Who we are

With more than 10 years of experience, Keith & Co Consulting (Pty) Ltd has been delivering some of the best project management in the event management industry across South Africa. Our background and vast experience in event infrastructure and project management make us highly competitive and experts in the field.

We conceptualise, design and develop our products and services to provide quality results that meet global standards, which in turn benefits the client. We pride ourselves on materialising bespoke client ideas and being present from start to finish of every exhibition stand build. We are passionate about the work we produce and always aim for quality rather than quantity.

We understand that events are complex, dynamic and ever-changing, and that time pressure and workload increase as the actual event approaches. Our aim is to alleviate that pressure by organising and managing the structuring and dynamics of an event on behalf of our clients.
